## 2026-10-05 — Rounding across rows of one invoice: the settling row takes the base remainder (MON-04, MON-06)
- **Superseded in part by:** 2026-10-05 "Cumulative rounding across rows of one invoice (supersedes the settling-row remainder) and confirmation of the unlink/delete refusal (MON-02, MON-04, MON-06)". The settling-row remainder (P1–P2, C1–C3, R1–R3) and its known limit are replaced by cumulative rounding. Still in force: the invoice's frozen `fx_rate` and `base_currency` on each row, computation once inside the invoice lock, and the status-filtered dashboard formula.
- **Decision:** Reconcile. Credit notes, payments and refunds keep the invoice's frozen `fx_rate` and `base_currency`, and each base amount is computed inside the invoice lock, once, and stored. The row that settles an invoice takes the remainder of its frozen base total. "Settles" means the document-currency net outstanding `amount_owed − net_paid` goes from > 0 to ≤ 0. The cases:
  - A settling payment stores `B_inv − B_cn − B_net_before`, plus any overpayment converted on its own (P1); other payments are converted on their own (P2).
  - A credit note that brings `amount_owed` to 0 stores base total `B_inv − B_cn_before` (C1). One that otherwise settles the invoice stores the remainder plus its converted excess (C2). Others are converted on their own (C3). Only the credit note's base total follows these cases; its parts stay converted on their own.
  - A refund that takes net paid to 0 stores the remaining `B_net` (R1). One that brings the excess to 0 on a non-void invoice stores `B_net − (B_inv − B_cn)` (R2). Others are converted on their own (R3).

Known limit: a credit-note void that leaves a `paid` invoice with exactly no excess, after further payments or partial refunds on it while settled, can leave a few minor units unreconciled; status excludes the invoice. - **Alternatives rejected:** independent per-row rounding with no reconciliation (the revision 3 default); refunds reversing base amounts proportionally to their payment, or at the payment row's base amount (neither keeps sums exact once a settling row carries a remainder); adjusting frozen credit-note snapshots after issue.
- **Scope / affects:** spec Status, "Changes in v4.6 revision 4" item 2, Open question 16, Money rules ("Base amounts for credit notes, payments and refunds"), Dashboard v1, Phase 5, Verification (j), (n), (p); `agents/PROJECT.md` §9; `packages/core/src/money` (future build).
