- Category: defect in documentation.
- Rule: FILE-03 exception review and AUTH-03.

The origin handler checks Owner membership and the current key at `apps/web/src/app/(app)/files/payment-qr/route.ts:16–28`, then sends successful responses with `Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600` at `:37`. This finding concerns the accuracy of the new record, based on the existing response header and the standard. Browser behavior was not exercised in this round.
- Required correction: State that the Owner and current-key checks apply at the origin, and document the successful response’s one-hour private cache lifetime.
- Owner: builder.
- Verification: Compare the amended control against the handler, header and test scope.
- Disposition: before merge.
- Status: open.
