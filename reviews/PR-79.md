#### [P2] Scope the QR access statement to requests that reach the server

- Category: defect in documentation.
- Rule: FILE-03 exception review and AUTH-03.
- Evidence: `docs/DECISIONS.md:14`, control (7), says the current QR is served only to an Owner and everything else returns 404. The origin handler checks Owner membership and the current key at `apps/web/src/app/(app)/files/payment-qr/route.ts:16–28`, then sends successful responses with `Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600` at `:37`. The tests at `apps/web/src/app/(app)/settings/payment/actions.test.ts:266–282` call the origin handler directly. [RFC 9111 §4.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111.html#section-4.2)
- Failure scenario: A browser can reuse a still-fresh successful QR response as allowed by its private cache. Reuse does not repeat the origin’s Owner or current-key checks.
- Why it matters: The exception record is the basis for the Phase 4 review. Its current language overstates the scope of verified access and freshness behavior. This finding concerns the accuracy of the new record, based on the existing response header and the standard. Browser behavior was not exercised in this round.
- Required correction: State that the Owner and current-key checks apply at the origin, and document the successful response’s one-hour private cache lifetime.
- Owner: builder.
- Verification: Compare the amended control against the handler, header and test scope.
- Disposition: before merge.
- Status: open.
