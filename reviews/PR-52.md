### [P1] The accent-contrast refusal rule can never fire, so the confirmed contrast control is empty
- Category: defect (in the plan) / guardrail mismatch
- Rule: A11Y-02 (`guardrails/frontend.md:58`: contrast ≥ 4.5:1 text, ≥ 3:1 UI components); `FRONTEND_STANDARDS.md` §3 rule 5
- Evidence: plan Task 6 line 148 ("`pickForeground(hex)` (black or white, whichever is higher) … refuse a hex where neither black nor white reaches 4.5:1"); plan Settled item 4 (line 60); DECISIONS 2026-11-04 entry added in this PR ("a refusal when neither reaches 4.5:1"). I computed `min over all luminances of max(contrast with white, contrast with black)` = **4.585** (equal at relative luminance ≈ 0.179). For every possible hex the better of black and white reaches at least 4.58:1, so the refusal branch is unreachable.
- Failure scenario: (a) the planned test "a low-contrast hex is refused with a message linked through `aria-describedby`" (Task 6, line 150) cannot be written: no hex is refused; (b) the real exposure is not covered: the accent also appears as a button/badge/focus-ring/link colour on the page background in both light and dark mode, where A11Y-02 needs ≥ 3:1 against the adjacent colour (e.g. a pale yellow `#ffe066` accent fails on a white card; a dark navy fails on the dark background). Nothing in the plan checks that, and the picker would show the owner no problem.
- Why it matters: Charlie confirmed "AA text on the accent, refuse if neither reaches 4.5:1" on the belief that it protects readability; it protects only text printed on the accent, and never refuses anything.
- Required correction: replace the rule with one that can fail and covers both themes, for example: foreground chosen automatically (black or white), **plus** the accent must reach ≥ 3:1 against the page background and card colour in light and in dark, else the colour is refused (or a theme-specific adjusted variant is derived and shown to the owner); fix the Task 6 tests to match; add a superseding DECISIONS line; Charlie re-confirms because his earlier answer rested on the unreachable rule.
- Owner: builder (Charlie re-confirms)
- Verification: unit test with a pale and a dark hex that is refused or adjusted; e2e shows the refusal message; the rule text in the plan and DECISIONS no longer says "neither black nor white reaches 4.5:1".
- Status: open

### [P2] Audit hashes of bank account numbers are brute-forceable
- Category: material risk (security; spec-decided design)
- Rule: spec line 799 ("account numbers stored in the audit entry as last 4 digits + SHA-256 of the full value"); plan Task 14 line 223
- Evidence: a bank account number has a small search space (about 10–16 digits, structured); an unsalted SHA-256 of it can be recovered by brute force by anyone who can read `audit_log`. The plan implements the spec as written and does not mention this.
- Why it matters: the aim is "who changed what" without storing the number; the hash adds little protection against a reader of the audit log.
- Required correction: raise it with Charlie as a spec question; the plan can propose an HMAC-SHA-256 with a server-side secret (still a SHA-256 construction) and add the secret to the env/secret handling. Not a plan-approval blocker if Charlie accepts the spec wording as is, recorded.
- Owner: owner (Charlie), builder drafts the proposal
- Verification: DECISIONS entry with Charlie's choice before PR 5 starts.
